## Available project-material findings

- Green Bags UAE and BWMC were publicly checked in Chrome on 2026-08-27. Their current homepages differ materially from repository visuals, so those visuals must not be described as current screenshots or before/after proof.
- No GA4/GSC export, ad-platform report, CRM export, campaign summary, signed testimonial, client approval, or dated before/after evidence was found.
- `_docs/fazil-prd.docx` is a marketing specification with placeholder/example metrics. It is not evidence.

## Publication gate

Every future numerical outcome must include source/reporting platform, measurement/comparison periods, baseline, final value, exact metric definition, attribution context, documented scope, and publication approval.

Paid advertising additionally requires spend, conversion definition, attribution window, and reporting source. SEO additionally requires Search Console and/or GA4 evidence, comparison period, and branded versus non-branded context. Until the full package is archived, use only documented scope, implementation, deliverables, and objectives.
