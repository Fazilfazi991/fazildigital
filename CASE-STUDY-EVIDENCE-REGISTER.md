## Portfolio evidence observations

- Named portfolio listings exist for Green Bags UAE, Golden Point, Zap Mart, BWMC, Bags Basket and Budget Home Fix.
- Repository visuals exist for those six named listings, but a visual alone does not prove delivery, authorship or results.
- Green Bags UAE and BWMC were checked in Chrome on 2026-08-27. Both public sites resolved, but their current homepages differ materially from the repository visuals. The visuals must not be represented as current live screenshots or before/after evidence.
- No analytics exports, ad-platform reports, signed testimonials, client approvals or dated before/after files were found in the repository.

## Publication rule

Do not classify a case-study performance claim as verified until its evidence package contains the source platform, reporting dates, baseline, final value, attribution method where relevant, project scope, and permission to publish.
