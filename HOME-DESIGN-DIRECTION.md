# Fazil Digital homepage direction

## Owner direction and scope

The owner rejected the generic blue/lime and cinematic direction. Follow the supplied photographer-portfolio reference for visual energy, not literal structure: bold editorial type, purposeful overlaps, orange portrait framing, off-white canvas and moments of deep black. Preserve the real owner portrait, verified biography, Saudi SEO content and technical infrastructure. Use natural page scrolling only. No scroll storytelling, artificial page height, auto-play or invented proof.

## Palette and type

Use approximately 65% off-white `#F8F7F3`, 25% black `#080808` / charcoal `#414141`, and 10% orange `#FFB638`, with neutral `#E7E5E1` and white. Orange is an accent and CTA surface with black text; white text on orange is not allowed. Use the existing Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Sans pair unless a real layout comparison demonstrates a better match. Typography should be bold and tightly tracked for display headings with clear, calm body text.

## Two first-screen concepts

### A — Portrait in an Orange Aperture (selected)

Off-white 5/7 split. The left side carries “Hey, I’m Mohammad Fazil,” the exact clear offer “I help businesses get found on Google,” a concise Saudi support line, primary “Let’s discuss your SEO” and secondary “Explore my work” actions. The right side features the real café portrait, framed by a rounded orange backing plane, without text covering the face. Place the Dubai / Saudi service area beneath the copy. On mobile, compose in reading order: intro, headline, offer, CTA, location, portrait. The offer and first action should be visible before the image starts.

### B — The Editorial Cover

A black hero slab with oversized off-white headline, orange kicker, short supporting line and clear action beside a cropped owner portrait inside an off-white/orange poster frame. Follow with a light service index. This uses the reference’s magazine contrast and scale, but risks delaying the offer and merging face with headline content.

### Comparison and choice

| Measure | A — Orange Aperture | B — Editorial Cover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First impression | Personal, confident, approachable | Dramatic, art-directed |
| Offer clarity | Excellent; large headline on calm solid ground | Good, but competing poster elements can crowd it |
| Trust | Direct portrait and direct-consultant introduction | Strong portrait, more stylized first impression |
| CTA prominence | Obvious black button beside supporting work link | Must compete with dark editorial composition |
| Mobile | Natural text-first stack | Requires careful stacked poster crop |
| Distinctiveness | Orange frame and bold display type create a recognizable signature | More novel but more visually demanding |

Choose A as the stronger conversion-first foundation. Borrow B’s black/orange/off-white editorial contrast for lower sections so the page still has a visual rhythm and character. The owner’s supplied screenshot is inspiration, not an asset or a layout to copy.

## Page sequence

1. Hero and owner portrait.
2. Short positioning line: marketing mindset, technology perspective, business focus.
3. Black personal introduction with a genuine monochrome portrait and verified biography link.
4. Interactive three-problem selector, with practical checks and next step.
5. Visually designed, clearly illustrative work formats (technical review, search-intent map, organic growth roadmap). No fabricated client data or outcomes.
6. Varied service bento with technical SEO prominent and links to existing service pages.
7. Four-step click-to-expand process.
8. Saudi locations (Riyadh primary, Jeddah, Dammam); no office claims.
9. Three editorial links to existing articles.
10. Contact CTA, privacy-safe WhatsApp message preparation and FAQs.

Use restrained hover and click feedback, accessible keyboard operation, reduced-motion support and normal document flow. Calicut remains `noindex, follow` and outside sitemap. Keep About facts, canonical URLs, redirects, analytics, service content, chatbot backend/privacy protections, and all existing SEO infrastructure.
