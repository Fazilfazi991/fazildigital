- Removed invented client counts, campaign samples, capacity claims, revenue/conversion lifts, numerical platform benchmarks and time-saving outcomes. Examples and allocation frameworks now identify their illustrative status where retained.
- Removed blanket PDPL, data-residency, accessibility, Arabic-language capability and certification guarantees. Tool hosting or retrieval alone does not establish legal compliance or accurate answers.
- Corrected claims that FAQ markup, `llms.txt`, fixed word counts, model personalities or a 48-hour deadline guarantee AI citations. Google's ordinary search eligibility and useful-content guidance remain the relevant foundation.
- Corrected directory/Maps advice: evaluate eligible channels and real business signals; do not present a mandatory directory set, exact punctuation matching or scripted review keywords as ranking requirements.
- Removed claims that Arabic and English are universally mandatory, that translation alone produces local relevance, or that city/sector demographics guarantee a platform's performance.
- Corrected CAC versus cost-per-lead language, legal/signature certainty, universal media-budget percentages and unlimited/infinite ROI promises.
- Rechecked historical Claude product descriptions against first-party material. Kept launch context and useful evaluation criteria, removed unsupported export, benchmark, autonomy and compliance conclusions, and linked current product guidance.
- Updated substantive article summaries as well as visible content so structured summaries do not repeat the removed assertions. Preserved original publication dates instead of implying newly published historical articles.
