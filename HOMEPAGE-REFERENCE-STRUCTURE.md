# Homepage reference structure review

Reference: [AyishaMuneer.com](https://www.ayishamuneer.com/)  
Review date: 11 November 2026  
Viewports inspected in the browser: 1270 × 713 desktop, 768 px tablet, and 390 × 844 mobile.

## Reference page sequence

1. **Header and navigation** — personal wordmark at left, a six-link desktop navigation, and a compact menu control on mobile.
2. **Hero and enquiry** — two-column desktop composition: personal headline and positioning copy at left; a multi-step enquiry form with a WhatsApp alternative at right.
3. **Personal introduction and credibility** — short biography followed by experience and outcome counters, then a profile link.
4. **Recent work** — horizontally browsable project gallery.
5. **Expertise** — selectable service list with an adjacent service detail panel; additional service links follow.
6. **Supporting business system** — a full-width CRM section connects the primary offer with business operations.
7. **Working process** — a short sequence with a direct discussion CTA.
8. **Client credibility strip** — a logo band.
9. **Audience fit** — three audience profiles, each with a distinct challenge and response.
10. **Conversion section** — prominent closing headline, reassurance, and contact actions.
11. **Testimonials** — quote and attribution groups.
12. **FAQs** — stacked expandable questions.
13. **Contact details and form** — contact information, map, and enquiry form.
14. **Footer** — personal identity, concise positioning, and grouped links.

## Composition and responsive notes

The page moves from an immediate enquiry opportunity into personal context and proof, then through work, expertise, supporting operations and process before qualifying visitors. It closes on conversion and offers detailed reassurance through testimonials, FAQs and contact options. Sections alternate between editorial copy, a browsable work gallery, selectable service details, and grouped information instead of repeating one card grid.

At desktop width, the first screen has a horizontal navigation and a balanced, left-aligned hero beside a large enquiry panel. The work section uses horizontal controls. The services area pairs compact choices with a detail panel. At 390 px, the header collapses to a menu control and the work gallery remains horizontally browsable; service choices compact into a two-column selection area with details below. At the observed 768 px tablet width, part of the desktop navigation was clipped, so Fazil Digital explicitly switches to its mobile menu before that width can overflow.

The reference's counters, client marks, services, testimonials, imagery, copy and branding are not reused. Fazil Digital replaces them with verified career context, clearly labelled examples of SEO deliverables, six focused SEO capabilities, a search-to-enquiry illustration, a six-stage onboarding sequence, and its existing privacy-safe review flow. No performance results, customer relationships or credentials are inferred from the reference.
