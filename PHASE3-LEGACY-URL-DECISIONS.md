## Approval package before a URL-level change

For each pending URL, collect the E1–E6 evidence referenced in its row. A proposed approval must name the exact source, action, destination where relevant, intent rationale, source exports and dates, valuable material to retain, implementation checks and rollback. A generic approval to “clean up legacy SEO” is insufficient for these pending URLs.

- **CONSOLIDATE:** demonstrate meaningful intent overlap, choose the destination from current evidence, preserve useful unique content, test a substantial destination with HTTP 200/self-canonical and approve one direct permanent mapping. Do not send unrelated pages to the Saudi SEO homepage. [Google migration guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/site-move-with-url-changes), [redirect guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/301-redirects).
- **NOINDEX:** establish why an accessible reference page should leave search, assess current visits/links and obtain URL-specific approval. A noindex rule needs to be crawlable to be seen; it is not implemented through robots.txt disallow. [Google noindex guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing).
- **RETIRE:** confirm that the business no longer offers the subject, there is no useful search/link/conversion value or substantive reference purpose, and no genuinely equivalent destination. Approve an intentional removal/404 or 410 and its monitoring/rollback; do not invent a homepage redirect.
- **KEEP:** maintain the exact URL, meaningful intent and factual copy. Reconsider later only with current evidence; protect source-supported historical assets from arbitrary country/topic cleanup.
