Prepared 6 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta). Phase 1 research and planning only; no internal page, redirect or article change is authorized by this document.

It is user-supplied, not live Search Console access. `SEO-URL-REGISTER.md` contains a separate historical 25 May–24 August 2026 snapshot. Research here combines that repository evidence, inspection of existing content and a live web-search sample on 6 November 2026. The search tool is not a controlled Saudi Google SERP, a rank tracker, Keyword Planner, Search Console export or customer interview. Results vary by location, language, device and time. No search volumes, keyword difficulty, CPCs, market shares or attainable rankings are established. Search-result presence validates a visible content pattern, not customer demand or a competitor's claims.

## Content clusters and editorial priorities

Each proposed article must solve a question distinct from the commercial page, have a useful primary owner and link to the relevant service. These are editorial briefs, not existing publications or promised results.

| Sequence | Proposed original article | Reader job | Service relationship and differentiation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Why your Saudi business is not appearing for relevant searches | Separate indexing problems, poor intent fit and weak local visibility | Technical SEO; show a practical diagnosis checklist rather than a second service pitch |
| 2 | Arabic and English SEO: how to decide which pages need localization | Choose content by audience need and independently researched search intent | Content strategy + technical SEO; include native review, RTL QA and genuine alternate-page requirements |
| 3 | Google Business Profile or website SEO: what does your business need? | Understand local search versus broader organic discovery | Local SEO; explain eligibility and accurate business information rather than false city profiles |
| 4 | What to include in an SEO brief for a Riyadh business | Compare providers on scope, access, reporting and responsibility | Riyadh SEO; buyer guidance without ranking promises or invented price bands |
| 5 | What should an SEO report tell a Saudi business owner? | Connect search visibility, relevant visits and qualified enquiries | SEO overview; include definitions and caveats, not a fake dashboard |
| 6 | Planning content for a longer B2B buying journey in the Eastern Province | Answer buyer questions with verifiable service and product detail | Dammam SEO + content strategy; treat industry fit as a hypothesis until client evidence exists |

Google advises original, useful, trustworthy content for an intended audience and cautions against producing many unrelated pages primarily for search traffic. The plan therefore expands useful topics deliberately rather than regenerating the old service-by-city matrix. [Google people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

The local SEO cluster should explain complete, accurate business information and the roles of relevance, distance and prominence. No service can promise to override those factors. [Google local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)

Use the supplied headline “SEO & Organic Growth for Saudi Businesses.” and supplied supporting copy. Explain the commercial problem through relevant search demand, local discovery, technical barriers and useful content. Name technical SEO, local SEO and SEO content strategy. State that Arabic content needs genuine localization and native-language review. Explain direct work with Mohammad through responsibility, communication and reporting commitments, not unsupported credentials or client counts.

FAQ answers should explain that timelines depend on the starting point and competition; fees depend on scope, site size, content needs and implementation; local SEO depends on how and where a business actually serves customers; Arabic strategy needs independent research; and work starts with a review before a scoped plan. No fixed ranking window, price range or outcome is established. Google similarly warns against guaranteed first-place claims when hiring an SEO. [Google hiring guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo)
