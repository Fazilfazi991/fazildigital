## Research and claim boundaries

Primary references were checked on 6 November 2026 and linked in the content where relevant:

- [Google's SEO hiring guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo): evaluating methods, scope and unsupported ranking promises.
- [Google Business Profile representation rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) and [local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en): actual operations, eligibility and local visibility limits.
- [Localized-version guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions): relationships between genuine published equivalents.
- [People-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [crawlable links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable): useful original material and meaningful related destinations.
- [Search Console Performance](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en), [URL Inspection](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9012289?hl=en) and [GA4 events](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9322688): different measurements and their limits.
- [Canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls), [robots guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots/intro) and [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): technical controls and lab/field distinctions.

No source was used to invent a business certification, office, fee, client relationship, review, ranking improvement or financial result. New content uses no guaranteed result dates, fixed payback claims or unsupported market statistics. A hypothetical CTR arithmetic example is identified as hypothetical. The service copy describes the intended engagement and responsibilities, not proof of completed delivery.

English content is implemented; Arabic drafting, native editorial approval and publication remain pending. Current live GSC exports, backlink evidence, approved numerical case outcomes and Saudi office/registration evidence are not available. They were not manufactured or implied by schema-oriented copy.

## Focused legacy credibility remediation

The lead additionally assigned known unsupported claims in existing article data. All original slugs and ordering were preserved: 10 in `blogs-cluster-1.ts`, 20 in `blogs-cluster-3.ts`, and 2 in `blogs-cluster-4.ts`, verified against the development branch's starting `HEAD`.

In `blogs-cluster-1.ts`, the pass corrected unsupported deployment/audit counts, credentials, saved-hours and conversion badges, campaign cohort and benchmark claims, blanket legal/API-retention assertions, guaranteed ROI, cost/lift estimates, attribution ratios and related metadata. It retained the topics and URL intent. Two price-led titles were changed to scope-based titles because their claimed prices were not supported. Meaningful edits are dated 6 November 2026.

The existing March 2026 core-update article retained its URL and topic but was rewritten to separate verified release dates from speculation. [Google's incident record](https://status.search.google.com/incidents/7eTbAa2jWdToLkraZj5y) supports the 27 March–8 April rollout. The article no longer claims a new E-E-A-T ranking factor, a preferred word count, an audited UAE/KSA impact study or a fixed recovery schedule. Its assessment framework links to [official core-update guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-updates).

The narrow `blogs-cluster-3.ts` correction removed the Riyadh “tripled the conversion rate” assertion and clarified the linked project note's evidence boundary. In `blogs-cluster-4.ts`, the known design-to-development “60%” claim family was removed across the excerpt, summary, introduction, table, list and first-person promotion; the associated unverified client count and automatic compliance implication were softened.

This is **not** certification of the complete legacy archive. Remaining material outside the approved focused corrections includes uncited model benchmarks and capability claims in `blogs-cluster-4.ts`, an unrelated numerical returns-review claim, a compliance-review percentage, provider feature assertions, regional market generalizations and older campaign advice. Those require their own source and editorial review. They remain preserved, rather than being deleted or deindexed without evidence. The new featured Saudi editorial experience does not present these legacy assertions as verified evidence for Fazil Digital.
