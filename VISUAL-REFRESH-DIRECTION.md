# Visual refresh direction and hero explorations

## Selected direction

**Editorial practice, with SEO work made visible.** Combine the shorter, more composed B headline and architectural visual with C's explanatory sample-review language. Keep the familiar forest/ivory brand and use the provided original art where available. An illustrative scene is a design asset, not a claim to show an actual location or office. The output should feel like a working consultancy's carefully edited publication.

- **Hero:** visible primary action with the existing search intent. Hold the exact core message “SEO & Organic Growth for Saudi Businesses.” at the center of the implementation. Keep direct work with Mohammad legible. Do not bury CTA below a giant decorative graphic.
- **Evidence:** a labelled illustrative review shows areas and prioritized outputs, never a result, ranking, client name or invented analytics screenshot.
- **Typography:** keep Manrope for navigation and body; keep Instrument Serif as controlled display voice rather than using a gigantic headline for every section. Set ordinary body copy near 16–18px, 1.5–1.7 line height, and comfortable reading measures around 45–75 characters. Scale headings fluidly and preserve readable mobile line breaks.
- **Composition:** alternate full-width editorial lines, asymmetric visual/copy pairings, compact service lists and one substantial dark section. Cards are reserved for meaningful grouped information, not a universal section shell. City, services, About, Blog and Contact should answer different visitor needs.
- **Color:** paper `#F5F3ED`, forest `#1E4032`, charcoal `#222B28`, muted sage, and sparing sand. Check contrast on actual rendered text; avoid using sand as small copy.
- **Interaction:** use restrained motion that clarifies hierarchy; preserve visible focus, touch targets, reduced-motion settings and semantic reading order. Keep the existing privacy-safe form event semantics and clean WhatsApp destination.
- **Representation:** Saudi market focus is legitimate; a physical Saudi office, verified client, credential, portrait or performance outcome is not established and must not be invented.

## Three review-only hero boards

These are static design studies, not public pages, finished assets, or evidence of a real client project. They intentionally vary their composition and hierarchy.

- [A — Human Expert-Led](design/hero-concepts/a-expert.svg): centered text-led statement above three consultant/focus/market facts. Strong personal framing, but no portrait is assumed.
- [B — Premium Editorial](design/hero-concepts/b-editorial.svg): left-aligned service message beside a distinctive architectural editorial visual, with a small layered statement. Best first impression; the shape is illustrative rather than documentary.
- [C — SEO in Action](design/hero-concepts/c-methodology.svg): a practical methodology and explicitly illustrative deliverable; gives business visitors a concrete sense of the work.

B is the default visual composition; use C's explicit, truthful deliverable structure elsewhere on the homepage. A informs the About page's direct-consultant voice. The implementation remains the authoritative design, and this proposal should be reconciled with actual rendered output after integration.
