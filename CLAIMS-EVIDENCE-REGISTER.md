## Evidence standard

A future numeric claim requires a case identifier, source, measurement period, baseline, final value, metric definition, attribution context, scope, and publication approval. Paid-media claims also require spend, conversion definition, attribution window, and reporting source. SEO claims require GA4/Search Console evidence, comparison period, and branded/non-branded context. Ratings require the public platform, review count, score, and review date.
