## Factual reference points

- Google's local results are primarily based on relevance, distance and prominence: [Google Business Profile local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en-en).
- Business Profile eligibility depends on in-person customer contact; online-only businesses are not eligible: [Google eligibility guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en).
- Address and service-area details must reflect the real business: [Google representation guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2853879?hl=en).
- Useful content should be made for people: [Google people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
- For measurement, Search Console supports page, query, country and device dimensions: [Performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en).
